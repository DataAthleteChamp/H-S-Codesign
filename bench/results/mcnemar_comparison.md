## Caveats

- n=60 captures; the asymptotic chi-square McNemar is unsafe at this n. We use `binomtest(min(b,c), n=b+c, p=0.5, two-sided)` as the exact form.
- Cluster bootstrap resamples captures, not file variants, so each capture contributes once per draw. The 95% CIs are wide as expected for small n.
- Both models are evaluated on the same 60 captures; pairing is at the capture level, which is the unit of independence.
